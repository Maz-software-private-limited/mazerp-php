This checklist keeps that chain in order—whether you are on spreadsheets today or comparing payroll software India options like index.php">MazERP Payroll . 1. Close the attendance and leave window Before you touch salary numbers, freeze the period. Confirm working days, holidays, approved leave, and any loss of pay (LOP) with managers. Late changes are the main reason payroll gets rerun. Cut-off date communicated to all locations Overtime and shift adjustments approved in writing LOP and half-day rules applied consistently 2. Validate employee and salary master data New joiners, exits, and salary revisions should be updated before the run. Check that each employee is on the right salary structure—components, effective dates, and bank details. 3. Run gross-to-net calculations Calculate earnings and deductions from your structures. This is where monthly payroll work either stays predictable or turns into manual fixes row by row. Typical lines Indian teams reconcile every month: PF (Provident Fund) employer and employee shares where applicable ESI where the establishment and wages fall under ESI rules TDS on salary per employee tax declarations and slabs Other deductions: loans, benefits, or internal recoveries 4. Review before you approve Finance and HR should scan exceptions first: new hires, full-and-final settlements, negative net pay, and anyone with unusual LOP or overtime. Compare totals to the prior month and investigate large swings. Approval is your control point. Once leadership signs off, treat the run as the source of truth for payouts and payslip communication. 5. Publish payslips and payment files Generate payslips that match the approved run. Employees should see the same gross, deductions, and net pay you approved—without retyping figures into PDFs. Export bank or payment instructions from the same dataset so accounting does not maintain a parallel sheet. 6. File and hand off statutory records Keep registers and supporting data your CA or compliance team needs for PF, ESI, and TDS filings. Even when filing happens outside payroll, the monthly run should produce reconcilable numbers. When spreadsheets stop scaling Spreadsheets work until headcount, locations, or statutory complexity make every month a reconciliation project.
